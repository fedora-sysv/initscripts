# Contributing

initscripts is a legacy project maintained primarily for compatibility. Keep
changes focused and avoid unrelated cleanups.

You must personally understand the changes you submit: why they are needed, how
they work, and what other behavior they may affect. AI-assisted contributions
are welcome, but generated explanations and passing tests do not replace your
understanding or responsibility for the patch.

For bug fixes, provide a reproducer, before-and-after results, and the tested
distribution, version, and configuration. Check side effects and compatibility.
State what remains unverified; do not present source inspection as reproduction.
Maintainers may decline changes that leave this investigation to reviewers.

If an agent added a review marker to `README.md`, remove it manually after
reviewing and understanding the complete patch, before submitting your PR.
